Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This is a translation; the French version prevails.

Salem is a role-playing Discord bot for the "L'antre de Salem" server only. This page explains which personal data it processes, why, for how long, and how to exercise your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act.

Data controller

Danaé Albrecht--Martin, reachable at contact@danaedev.fr.

Data stored by the bot

The bot only creates a game account when you use one of its game commands. It then stores:

Data used but not stored

  • Your Discord name and avatar, read when you use a command to show your profile.
  • Your server roles, to check your rights (member, beta, moderation).
  • The time and ID of your latest messages, kept in memory for a few seconds to detect flooding. The bot does not read the content of your messages.
  • The server's technical logs, which may hold your Discord ID and the name of a command you used: they are used to fix errors and are wiped at each bot update.

The bot sets no cookies, shows no ads and never sells or shares your data.

Retention periods

  • Game account: as long as you play; deleted on request, and at the latest 3 years after your last activity in the game.
  • Purchase records: while the subscription is active, then 3 years after it ends, to be able to answer a possible dispute. They are kept even if you delete your game account, with /reset or through the automatic deletion of inactive accounts.
  • Warnings and sanctions: 2 years from their date, then deleted. They are kept even if you delete your game account or leave the server, so that the rules still apply during that time.
  • Referral: 3 years after the arrival on the server, then deleted. It is kept if you leave the server, so that a former member who comes back does not become a godchild; a godmother's invite link and tier are deleted with her game account.
  • Welcoming newcomers: 3 years after the welcome card, then deleted. It is kept even if you delete your game account or leave the server, so that leaving and coming back gives no gold again.
  • Database backups: one copy a day, kept 30 days then deleted. Data removed from the database therefore also leaves the backups within 30 days.
  • Technical logs: until the next bot update. Between two updates, they are capped at 10 MB: the oldest lines are deleted as new ones come in.

Recipients and hosting

  • Access: only the data controller can access the database. Moderators see, on Discord, the sanctions they look up or apply.
  • Hosting of the bot and its database: a virtual private server rented from OVHcloud (OVH SAS, 2 rue Kellermann, 59100 Roubaix, France), located in the OVHcloud data centre in Singapore. This transfer outside the European Union is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses included in OVHcloud's data processing agreement.
  • Backups: stored by OVHcloud in its Strasbourg data centre, in France.
  • Discord: Discord sells the subscription and sends the purchase records to the bot. The bot's messages and replies also go through Discord, which processes them under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, erase or port your data, to restrict its processing, and object to processing based on legitimate interest.

  • Delete your game account: during the beta, the /reset compte command erases it at once, with your wands and items. Otherwise, ask the moderation team or send an email.
  • Other requests: write to contact@danaedev.fr with your Discord name. You will get an answer within one month.
  • Complaint: if you think your rights are not respected, you can contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

Minimum age

Discord requires you to be at least 13, or the minimum age in your country. The bot does not ask your age and does not track you outside the server.

Purchases

The Cercle des Mécènes subscription and future paid content are sold by Discord (see the Terms of Sale). Payment is made at Discord: the bot only receives the purchase records described above. It never receives your card or bank details.

Changes

Any significant change will be announced on the server, and the update date at the top of this page will change.
