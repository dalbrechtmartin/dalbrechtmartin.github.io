These terms describe Salem's "Cercle des Mécènes" subscription and the bot's future paid content. They add to the Terms of Service.

1. Discord is the reseller

Purchases are made only in Discord. Discord acts as the reseller of the offer: you pay Discord, not Salem's publisher. The sale itself is governed by the Discord Paid Services Terms.

Discord therefore handles:

  • payment and payment methods;
  • VAT and invoicing;
  • refunds and the right of withdrawal;
  • the parental permission required for people under 18.

Salem never sees your bank details.

2. The offer: the Cercle des Mécènes

The Cercle des Mécènes is a monthly subscription, at about €4.99 a month. The exact price is the one Discord shows at purchase.

It is first of all a way to support the project. It helps pay for hosting and for the time spent developing Salem. Today, it gives:

  • the ｢💎｣ VIP role on the L'antre de Salem server;
  • the epic chest on day 7 of /quotidien: the same rare chest as everyone else, plus one guaranteed amethyst (never more gold);
  • -50% on the profile cosmetics (backdrops and frames) of the shop, paid with gold earned in the game;
  • access to the VIP-only shop items when they come out. These items are bought with gold earned in the game.

Conditions to enjoy them:

  • the perks require being a member of the L'antre de Salem server: the VIP role is taken away if you leave the server and given back if you return during the subscription;
  • during the beta, the game commands (including /quotidien and the shop) can only be used by people who have access to the beta. The subscription does not give this access: without it, only the VIP role can be used.

More perks will be added over time. They will stay in the same spirit:

  • no power advantage over other players;
  • no random paid content;
  • no gold for money.

3. Duration and cancellation

The subscription renews every month until you stop it. To stop it: in Discord, open User Settings > Subscriptions, then cancel the subscription. The perks stay active until the end of the period already paid.

If you got the VIP role in another way than the subscription, the end of the subscription does not take it away.

4. Changes to the perks

If perks were to be removed or substantially changed, subscribers would be told before the change and could cancel their subscription, as Discord's terms require. A one-time purchase whose content is substantially changed would be refunded.

5. Beta reset

Salem is in beta and the game may be reset. If so, anything bought with real money is kept, or replaced by an equivalent in-game compensation.

6. Ban from the server or bot shutdown

The publisher cannot cancel a subscription herself: only the subscriber, in Discord, or Discord support can. In case of a ban from the server or if Salem shuts down for good, the publisher tells the subscribers so that they cancel in Discord, and asks Discord to stop the renewals. Any refund is governed by Discord's terms.

7. Contact and complaints

For any question about the offer or a perk you did not receive: contact@danaedev.fr. For a payment or a refund, also contact Discord support.
